About This Playground

Inspired by Henri Matisse’s cut-outs, this little playground invites everyone to explore the joy of colours, shapes, and imagination.

Pick a colour, draw a shape, cut a little paper, and see where your imagination takes you.

There are no right or wrong answers here. Just colours, shapes, and endless possibilities.

A Note of Appreciation

This project is a playful tribute to the creative spirit of Henri Matisse and his remarkable cut-paper artworks.

It is an independent creative project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any Matisse institution, foundation, or estate.

關於這座遊樂場

這座小小的創作遊樂場，受到亨利・馬諦斯剪紙藝術的啟發。我們邀請每個人透過色彩、形狀與想像力，享受自由創作的樂趣。

選一張喜歡的色紙、畫出一個形狀、剪下一片紙，看看想像力會帶你走到哪裡。

這裡沒有標準答案，只有色彩、形狀，以及無限的可能。
